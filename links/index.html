Projects Working Toward Real Democracy

Citizen Assemblies & Sortition

  • Sortition Foundation Promotes randomly selected citizen assemblies as a replacement for electoral elites.
  • newDemocracy Foundation Designs and implements deliberative citizen assemblies for public decision-making.
  • Assemblée Citoyenne French network advocating permanent citizen assemblies and constitutional reform.

Institutional & Constitutional Reform

  • Democracy Without Borders Works on transnational democratic governance beyond the nation-state.
  • Participedia Open database of participatory and deliberative democratic processes worldwide.
  • Deliberative Democracy Consortium Research and practice network for deliberative institutional design.

Radical & Post-Liberal Democratic Thought

  • Demarchy.org John Burnheim’s work on functional demarchy and non-electoral governance.
  • P2P Foundation Explores commons-based governance, peer production, and non-hierarchical coordination.
  • Commons Transition Institutional transition toward commons-based political and economic models.

Practical Civic Technology

  • Decidim Open-source platform for participatory democracy and collective decision-making.
  • Pol.is AI-assisted deliberation tool for mapping consensus in large groups.
