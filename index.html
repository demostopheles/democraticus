Democraticus Simulator

What is this?

This simulator will allow citizens to explore, test and understand how an authentic representative democracy actually functions.

Conceptual Basis

Democraticus proposes the first and only model of authentic representative democracy.

  • Permanent Civic Assembly drawn by lot
  • Binding oversight over executive, legislative, judicial powers
  • Public deliberation and multilingual transparency
  • Structural prevention of power capture
