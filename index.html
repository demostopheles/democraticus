Authentic Representative Democracy

From Homo Sapiens to Homo Democraticus.
The evolutionary leap in sovereignty.

Diagnosis

Today’s representative systems are not authentic democracies. They are oligarchies disguised as democracies.

The system is structurally designed to confiscate sovereignty while preserving the illusion of participation.

The Solution

  • Permanent Civic Assembly drawn by lot
  • Binding oversight over executive, legislative and judicial powers
  • Public deliberation and multilingual transparency
  • Structural prevention of power capture

Open Source & Global

The model is public, open-source, legally grounded, and globally applicable.

No parties. No leaders. No funding requests. Only replication.
