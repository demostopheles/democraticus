About Democraticus

Democraticus is a project aimed at designing and testing an authentic form of representative democracy.

Purpose

The project explores how democratic legitimacy can be restored through institutional design, structural safeguards, and permanent citizen oversight.

Scope

This platform hosts theoretical models, simulations, assemblies, and tools to collectively refine a post-electoral democratic architecture.
