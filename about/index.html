About Democraticus

From Homo Sapiens to Homo Democraticus
The Evolutionary Leap in Sovereignty

Diagnosis

Today’s representative systems are not authentic democracies.
They are oligarchies disguised as democracies.

Professional politicians, political parties, lobbies, and debt-based finance have captured the sovereignty that constitutionally belongs exclusively to citizens.
Elections have become a periodic ritual that legitimizes betrayal: citizens choose who will ignore their interests until the next vote.

This is not the fault of individual politicians.
It is a structural flaw deliberately embedded in the mechanism of representation without permanent citizen control.

The Solution

Democraticus proposes the first and only model of authentic representative democracy.

Representatives continue to be elected, but they are placed under the permanent, direct, and binding supervision of a supreme Civic Assembly composed of ordinary citizens drawn by lot.

This Civic Assembly is the highest institution of the State:

  • It oversees and controls the three powers (executive, legislative, judicial)
  • It has the power to veto laws, revoke mandates, and initiate binding legislation
  • It deliberates publicly and in multiple languages
  • It structurally prevents the capture of power by parties, lobbies, or financial interests

Sovereignty returns fully and permanently to citizens without eliminating the advantages of representation.

Open-Source Revolution

The complete model is public and open-source (Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0).
Full book (660 pages), summaries, legal texts ready for adoption, simulator, and all documents are freely available to download, translate, adapt, and implement.

No leaders. No parties behind the project. No requests for funding.
Only a clear invitation to humanity to complete the democratic evolution.
