Major Treatises & Books

The Metacognitive Species (165 pages)

Autopsia della democrazia rappresentativa (584 pagine)

A comprehensive forensic investigation into modern political power that systematically dismantles the myth of representative democracy[cite: 3].

The Troy Syndrome (49 pages)

A trenchant examination of internal institutional decay and cognitive capture within modern social architectures[cite: 3].

Institutional System Engineering (257 pages)

An applied systems-engineering approach to constitutional public law, civic sovereignty, and institutional design[cite: 3].

Architetture della Democrazia (175 pages)

A historical and theoretical volume analyzing institutional models of direct citizen power across human civilization[cite: 3].

The Irreducible Minority Hypothesis (76 pages)

An evolutionary, cybernetic, and network-theory examination of human conformity, dissent, and social phase transitions[cite: 3].

Democrazia a misura di bambino (e di adulto) (38 pages)
